Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to use this guide?
No! The guide is designed for complete beginners. It starts with Scrum fundamentals and progressively builds to advanced topics. No prior Agile experience or technical background required to begin your certification journey.
Which certification should I get first?
Most beginners start with PSM I (Scrum.org) or CSM (Scrum Alliance). The guide covers both certifications in detail and helps you choose based on your career goals, budget, and preferred learning style. PSM I is often recommended as it never expires and has no renewal fees.
How long does it take to get certified?
Most people prepare in 2-4 weeks studying 5-10 hours per week. The guide includes structured study schedules and 250+ practice exam questions to accelerate your preparation and help you pass on your first attempt.
