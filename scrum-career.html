No! The guide is designed for complete beginners. It starts with Scrum fundamentals and progresses to advanced topics. No prior Agile experience required.

Most beginners start with PSM I (Scrum.org) or CSM (Scrum Alliance). The guide covers both and helps you choose based on your career goals and learning style.

Most people prepare in 2-4 weeks studying 5-10 hours per week. The guide includes study schedules and practice exams to accelerate your preparation.
