Last Updated: June 10, 2026
In Plain English:
We use affiliate links on Software Tool Guide. If you click one of our links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission — at absolutely no extra cost to you. This is how we keep our website free, our reviews honest, and our content updated regularly.

1. What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are special tracking URLs that identify when a reader clicks through our website and makes a purchase. When you click one of our affiliate links and buy a product, the software company pays us a small referral commission. This is a standard practice used by almost all review and comparison websites, including major publications like Wirecutter, TechRadar, and CNET.

Important: Using our affiliate links does NOT increase the price you pay. In fact, we often negotiate exclusive discounts and bonus packages for our readers that you won't find anywhere else.

2. Why We Use Affiliate Links

Running a quality review website requires significant resources. Affiliate commissions help us cover:

  • Research & Testing: We invest countless hours researching, testing, and comparing software tools to provide you with accurate, up-to-date information.
  • Content Creation: Our team includes experienced writers, editors, and industry experts who create detailed reviews, comparisons, and guides.
  • Website Maintenance: Hosting, security, design, and technical maintenance to keep Software Tool Guide fast, secure, and accessible worldwide.
  • Free Access: We believe valuable information should be free. Affiliate commissions allow us to keep our content freely available to everyone.
  • Continuous Updates: Software changes rapidly. We constantly update our reviews to reflect new features, pricing changes, and user feedback.

3. Our Commitment to Editorial Integrity

This is our promise to you: Our editorial integrity comes before everything else. We have strict policies in place to ensure our reviews remain unbiased and trustworthy:

  • No Pay-for-Play: We never accept payment, gifts, or incentives in exchange for positive reviews or higher rankings.
  • Independent Testing: Every tool we review is independently researched and tested by our team before we publish our findings.
  • Honest Pros & Cons: We always highlight both the strengths and weaknesses of every tool — even ones we earn commissions from.
  • Real User Feedback: We incorporate feedback from real users, review platforms, and industry experts into our assessments.
  • Regular Updates: If a tool changes (for better or worse), we update our review to reflect that — even if it affects our commissions.
  • Full Transparency: Every page containing affiliate links is clearly marked, and this disclosure is always accessible.
Our Philosophy:
"If we wouldn't recommend it to our best friend, we won't recommend it to you — regardless of commission rates."

4. Which Affiliate Programs Do We Participate In?

Software Tool Guide participates in several reputable affiliate programs, including but not limited to:

  • Digistore24 — Our primary affiliate network for digital products and software
  • Individual Software Vendor Programs — Direct partnerships with software companies

We only partner with programs that offer genuine value to our readers. We regularly review our affiliate partnerships and remove any that don't meet our quality standards.

5. Does Affiliate Status Affect Rankings?

No. Our rankings, scores, and recommendations are based solely on our evaluation criteria, which include:

  • Feature completeness and quality
  • Pricing and value for money
  • Ease of use and learning curve
  • Customer support quality
  • User reviews and community feedback
  • Regular updates and development activity
  • Integration capabilities

A tool's affiliate program status has zero influence on where it ranks in our comparisons or recommendations.

6. Does This Cost You Extra?

Absolutely not! The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our affiliate link or go directly to the vendor's website. In many cases, we've negotiated exclusive discounts, extended trials, or bonus packages that actually save you money compared to buying directly.

Using our affiliate links is a win-win: you get the best possible deal, and we earn a small commission that helps us continue providing free, high-quality content.
