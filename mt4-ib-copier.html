What is the MT4 to InteractiveBrokers Order Copier?

MT4 to InteractiveBrokers Order Copier is a professional trading automation software that instantly copies orders from MetaTrader 4 (MT4) to InteractiveBrokers (IB) Trader Workstation (TWS) in real-time. It's designed for traders who want to execute their MT4 strategies on IB's powerful brokerage platform without manual order entry.

The software works as a bridge between MT4 and IB TWS, ensuring that every trade placed in MT4 is automatically mirrored in your InteractiveBrokers account — including stop losses, take profits, and partial closes.
