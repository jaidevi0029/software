What is the MT4 to InteractiveBrokers Order Copier?

MT4 to InteractiveBrokers Order Copier is a professional trading automation software that instantly copies orders from MetaTrader 4 (MT4) to InteractiveBrokers (IB) Trader Workstation (TWS) in real-time.
