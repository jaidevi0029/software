Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a WordPress site to use DigiMember?
Yes, DigiMember is a WordPress plugin. You need a self-hosted WordPress site (WordPress.org) to install and use it. It works seamlessly with any WordPress theme and is compatible with all major page builders including Elementor, Gutenberg, and Divi.
Is this a one-time payment or subscription?
One-time payment of $97 for the standard license which includes 1 year of support and updates. Renewals are completely optional at a discounted rate. You can continue using the plugin even without renewing — it won't stop working.
What payment gateways are supported?
Stripe, PayPal, Braintree, Authorize.net, 2Checkout, Mollie, and 30+ others via seamless integration with popular WordPress payment plugins. You can connect multiple gateways simultaneously.
Can I migrate from another membership plugin?
Yes! DigiMember includes built-in import tools for MemberPress, LearnDash, WishList Member, and other popular membership plugins. The migration wizard makes transferring your members, courses, and subscriptions straightforward.
