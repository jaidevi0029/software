ChatGPT Prompts for Marketers: 100+ Templates That Actually Work

May 11, 2026 10 min read 6,789 views

Stop wasting time staring at a blank screen. These **100+ ChatGPT prompts** will help you generate marketing content in seconds. Just copy, paste, and customize.

Email Marketing Prompts

📧 Welcome Email Series (3 emails)

Write a 3-email welcome sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. First email: introduce brand and value. Second email: share a helpful tip. Third email: soft pitch with discount code. Tone: friendly and helpful.

📧 Abandoned Cart Recovery

Write an abandoned cart recovery email for [PRODUCT NAME]. Subject line: "You left something special behind". Body: remind them what they left, offer 10% discount code, create urgency (expires in 24 hours).

📧 Cold Outreach for B2B

Write a cold outreach email to [TARGET CUSTOMER]. Introduce my company [COMPANY NAME] that helps [SOLUTION]. Mention a specific problem they might have. Propose a 15-min call or demo. Keep under 150 words.

Social Media Prompts

📱 Instagram Caption

Write 5 Instagram captions for a post about [TOPIC]. Include 3-5 relevant hashtags. Tone: [casual/professional/funny]. Each caption under 150 characters.

📱 Twitter Thread

Create a Twitter thread about [TOPIC] with 5-7 tweets. Start with a hook, share valuable tips, end with a question. Keep each tweet under 280 characters.

📱 LinkedIn Post

Write a LinkedIn post sharing my experience about [TOPIC]. Format: personal story (2-3 sentences), lessons learned (2-3 bullets), call-to-action question. Professional tone, not salesy.

Ad Copy Prompts

🖥️ Facebook Ad Headline & Description

Create 10 Facebook ad headlines and descriptions for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Focus on [BENEFIT]. Each headline under 40 characters, description under 125 characters.

🖥️ Google Search Ad

Write 3 Google Search ads for keyword "[KEYWORD]". Include headline with keyword, description highlighting [BENEFIT] and [OFFER]. End with call-to-action: "Learn More" or "Sign Up".

Content & SEO Prompts

📝 Blog Outline

Create a detailed outline for a blog post titled "[TITLE]". Include H1, 5 H2s, and 3 subheadings under each H2. Add 2-3 bullet points under each subheading for content ideas.

📝 FAQ Section

Generate 10 FAQ questions and answers for a page about [TOPIC]. Each answer should be 1-2 sentences. Include a mix of beginner and advanced questions.

📝 Meta Description

Write 5 meta descriptions for a page about [TOPIC]. Include primary keyword "[KEYWORD]". Each under 160 characters. Focus on [BENEFIT] to encourage clicks.
💡 Pro Tip: Add "Write in the style of [brand voice]" at the end of your prompts. Example: "Write in casual, friendly tone similar to Mailchimp's style."
