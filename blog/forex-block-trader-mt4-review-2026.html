Understanding Institutional Block Trading

Before diving into the strategy details, it's essential to understand what institutional block trading means. Large financial institutions — banks, hedge funds, and investment firms — cannot enter or exit positions with a single click like retail traders. Their order sizes are so massive that they must accumulate or distribute positions gradually to avoid moving the market against themselves.

This accumulation and distribution process leaves footprints on price charts — areas where price tends to reverse or consolidate. These areas are called order blocks. The Forex Block Trader strategy is specifically designed to identify these order blocks and trade from them, effectively following the institutional money flow.
