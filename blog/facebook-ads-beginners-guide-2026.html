Facebook Ads Beginner Guide 2026: From Zero to First Sale

May 13, 2026 12 min read 3,456 views

Facebook Ads is one of the most powerful advertising platforms in 2026. With over 2.9 billion monthly active users, you can reach any audience. This step-by-step guide will teach you how to run your first Facebook ad campaign from scratch.

Step 1: Set Up Facebook Business Manager

Go to business.facebook.com and create your Business Manager account. Then add your Facebook Page and Ad Account. You also need to add a payment method.

Step 2: Understand Facebook Ad Structure

  • Campaign: Your objective (awareness, traffic, conversions, sales)
  • Ad Set: Targeting, budget, schedule, placement
  • Ad: Creative, headline, description, call-to-action

Step 3: Choose Your Campaign Objective

For Beginners: Start with "Traffic" or "Conversions" (if you have a website). "Traffic" sends people to your site. "Conversions" optimizes for purchases or signups.

Step 4: Define Your Target Audience

  • Location: Country, city, or radius around a location
  • Age & Gender: Who is your ideal customer?
  • Interests: Pages they like, topics they follow
  • Behaviors: Purchase behavior, device usage

Step 5: Set Your Budget

Daily budget: Facebook spends up to this amount each day. Start with $5-10/day. Lifetime budget: Total amount for entire campaign duration.

Step 6: Create Your Ad Creative

  • Format: Single image, carousel, video, collection
  • Primary text: The main message (125 characters recommended)
  • Headline: Catchy title (40 characters max)
  • Description: Optional extra text (30 characters)
  • Call-to-action: Learn More, Shop Now, Sign Up

Step 7: Install Facebook Pixel

The Facebook Pixel tracks conversions and builds audiences. Install it on your website via manual code, Google Tag Manager, or Shopify/WooCommerce plugins.

Step 8: Launch & Monitor

Click "Publish" and wait 24-48 hours for Facebook to optimize. Don't make changes too frequently. Let Facebook's algorithm learn.

Facebook Ads Checklist for Beginners

  • ✅ Start with $5-10 daily budget (don't overspend)
  • ✅ Target 1-2 interests (not too broad or narrow)
  • ✅ Use 1-2 ad images or videos per ad set
  • ✅ Run campaign for at least 3-5 days before judging results
  • ✅ Track cost-per-click (CPC) and cost-per-result
  • ✅ Improve click-through rate (CTR) with better creatives
💡 Pro Tip: Start with a "Video Views" campaign to build an audience, then retarget viewers with a "Conversions" campaign. This two-step strategy works great for beginners.
