What Does "4D Trading" Actually Mean?

The 4D Trader gets its name from analyzing four dimensions of market data simultaneously:

  • Dimension 1 - Price Action: Candlestick patterns and support/resistance levels.
  • Dimension 2 - Time Cycles: Identifies recurring time-based patterns when volatility typically increases.
  • Dimension 3 - Volume/Momentum: Measures the strength behind price moves to filter weak signals.
  • Dimension 4 - Volatility: Adapts SL/TP based on current market conditions (ATR-based dynamic levels).

By combining these four dimensions, the strategy aims to filter out noise and only generate signals when all dimensions align—similar to the multi-confluence approach used by professional institutional traders.
