How We Review Software

Our review process is thorough, transparent, and consistent across every product we evaluate:

  • Step 1: Research and shortlist the top tools in each category based on market presence and user feedback
  • Step 2: Personally test each software for 2-4 weeks minimum to understand real-world performance
  • Step 3: Compare features, pricing, ease of use, customer support, and real user reviews
  • Step 4: Write honest pros and cons based on actual testing — not marketing materials
  • Step 5: Continuously update reviews as software evolves with new features and pricing changes
